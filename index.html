How it works

A straightforward path from inquiry to steady-state operations.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    Share volumes, product type (ambient), preferred location, and timeline through the request form. No pitch theater — just the facts we need to assess fit.

  2. 2

    Scope & site fit

    We review capability against your flow (cross-dock, pick-and-pack, or other) and confirm which facility — Charlotte or Sidney — can support it.

  3. 3

    Agree on process

    Align on receiving rules, labeling, inventory handoffs, and reporting before the first shipment moves.

  4. 4

    Operate & refine

    Run day-to-day logistics with clear contacts, and adjust as your volume or mix changes.

Why Bamal

Modest facts from decades of industrial distribution — not marketing claims.

Industrial experience

Founded in 1953, Bamal Fastener is a privately held company with deep roots in industrial distribution. We understand dock schedules, lot control, and B2B replenishment — because we live them every day.

Dual-node network

Charlotte HQ on the I-85 / I-77 corridor and a Sidney, OH facility on I-75 — Midwest and Carolinas coverage under one partner. Additional sites across the U.S. and Mexico support the broader business.

Quality discipline

Bamal Fastener is registered to ISO 9001:2015. Locations pages note ISO 9001:2015 registered sites including Charlotte (HQ), the Chicago-area facility (La Grange, IL), and Sidney, OH. Ask your sales contact for current certificate details.

Practical 3PL expansion

We are extending our logistics capability into third-party services — starting with cross-docking and pick-and-pack for ambient goods — for shippers who want an industrial partner, not a DTC fulfillment brand.
